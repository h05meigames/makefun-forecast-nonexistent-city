Entry for the [Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-forecast-for-a-city-2a8769cf) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one line, at most 120 characters, that reads as a weather forecast for a city that does not exist — and is funny.

## The forecast

> Wobbleton forecast: heavy gravy showers after 6pm; residents advised to bring bread, not umbrellas.

**99 characters** (limit: 120).

The weather itself is the joke — rain made of gravy, heavy enough to need cover from — delivered in the flat voice of an official bulletin, right down to the standing advisory for residents. Wobbleton is a place where gravy rain is routine enough to plan lunch around.

## How it runs

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it with its character count.
